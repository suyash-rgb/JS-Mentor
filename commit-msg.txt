feat: Zero-Touch Automated FCFS Cohort Assignment (Max Capacity = 10 Students)

> **Zero-Touch Automated FCFS Cohort Assignment (Max Capacity = 10 Students)**:
> - No manual "Create Cohort" or "Auto-Assign" buttons exist in the UI.
> - Because our live classroom uses a **P2P WebRTC & Web Audio Mixer architecture (0 server cost)**, we cap each cohort at **10 students maximum** (recommended over 20). This ensures stable upload bandwidth (~3–4 Mbps total for the trainer), zero CPU throttling, and smooth streaming even for students on mobile data without WiFi.
> - Whenever a student registers or when unassigned students are detected by the backend, they are automatically allocated to an existing non-full cohort (`capacity < 10`) in First-Come, First-Served order.
> - If all existing cohorts are at capacity (10 students), a new cohort is created automatically and assigned to trainers in FCFS join order (earliest registered trainer with the fewest cohorts gets priority).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero-Touch Automated Daily Scheduling (4–7 PM IST Time-Slot Allocation)**:
> - No manual "Schedule Class" or "Auto-Schedule" buttons exist in the UI.
> - Our Doubt Scheduling algorithm (`app/services/scheduler.py`) strictly allocates 30-min/60-min sessions between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST** (the **10-4 Rule**).
> - To automate class scheduling and prevent any conflict with doubt sessions, **Group Online Classes** for today are automatically generated by the backend when needed in the **4:00 PM – 7:00 PM IST** window in sequential order of a trainer's cohorts:
>   - **Trainer's 1st Cohort** → automatically assigned **4:00 PM – 5:00 PM IST** (16:00 – 17:00)
>   - **Trainer's 2nd Cohort** → automatically assigned **5:00 PM – 6:00 PM IST** (17:00 – 18:00)
>   - **Trainer's 3rd Cohort** → automatically assigned **6:00 PM – 7:00 PM IST** (18:00 – 19:00)
> - Trainers simply open their dashboard and see today's scheduled classes ready to start.

## Proposed Changes

### Backend (`JS-Mentor-Backend/JS-Mentor` on branch `backend-37-online-classes`)

#### [NEW] [cohort_service.py](file:///d:/Apoliums%203/JS-Mentor-Backend/JS-Mentor/app/services/cohort_service.py)
- `auto_assign_students_fcfs(db: Session, max_capacity: int = 10)`:
  - Finds all students where `cohort_id IS NULL` ordered by `id ASC` (FCFS).
  - Finds cohorts with `< max_capacity` students and assigns students.
  - When all cohorts are full, picks the earliest trainer (`Trainer.id ASC`) with the minimum number of cohorts and creates a new cohort automatically.
- `ensure_today_classes_scheduled(db: Session, trainer_id: int)`:
  - Checks if today's classes are already scheduled for `trainer_id`'s cohorts.
  - If not, automatically maps each cohort sequentially to the dedicated IST evening slots:
    - Cohort index 0 → 16:00–17:00 IST (10:30–11:30 UTC)
    - Cohort index 1 → 17:00–18:00 IST (11:30–12:30 UTC)
    - Cohort index 2 → 18:00–19:00 IST (12:30–13:30 UTC)
  - Creates the `GroupClass` sessions for today and emits real-time `new-group-class-scheduled` notifications via Socket.IO.

#### [MODIFY] [trainer.py](file:///d:/Apoliums%203/JS-Mentor-Backend/JS-Mentor/app/routers/trainer.py)
- In `GET /api/v1/trainer/cohorts`:
  - Automatically invoke `auto_assign_students_fcfs(db)` and `ensure_today_classes_scheduled(db, trainer_id)` so the data is always up to date without any manual trigger endpoints.
  - Return the trainer's cohorts with their assigned student list and today's automatically scheduled `GroupClass` details.
